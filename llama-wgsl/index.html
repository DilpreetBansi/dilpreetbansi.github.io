llama-wgsl

A language model running on your GPU. Hand-written WGSL kernels, zero dependencies, no server.

Chat with a model that never leaves your machine.

llama-wgsl downloads SmolLM2 once, streams the weights straight into GPU memory, and runs every layer with compute shaders written by hand in WGSL. Pick a model and press Load.
